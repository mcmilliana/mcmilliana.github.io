#### \# Project Name: McMillian's Computer Services





#### \## Project Scope Statement

### 

#### \## Project Overview



The purpose of this project is to establish a reliable computer support service through McMillian’s Small Computer Business. The project will provide individuals with technical assistance for their personal desktop computers and laptops. Services will include diagnosing computer problems, improving system performance, installing and configuring software and hardware, resolving common hardware and operating-system issues, and helping customers maintain their devices.2. Project Scope \& Deliverables



#### \## Project Objectives



* Provide personal computer setup, installation, and configuration services.



* Diagnose and troubleshoot common hardware and software problems.



* Perform routine computer maintenance and system optimization.



* Install and configure operating systems, applications, printers, and other peripherals.



* Provide basic data backup, file transfer, and data recovery assistance.



* Help customers improve computer security through software updates, antivirus protection, and secure configuration.



* Provide personalized technical support and basic computer guidance to customers.



* Complete service requests accurately and within agreed-upon timeframes.



* Maintain clear records of services performed and recommendations provided.



* Deliver professional, dependable, and customer-focused computer services.





#### \## Project Scope

### 

##### \### In Scope



* Personal computer setup and initial configuration.



* Installation and configuration of operating systems.



* Installation and setup of commonly used software and applications.



* Computer hardware installation and replacement.



* Troubleshooting and repair of common hardware and software problems.



* Virus, malware, and unwanted software removal.



* System updates and performance optimization.



* Printer setup and configuration.



* Data backup and file transfer.



* Basic data recovery assistance.



* Basic computer security configuration.



* Network and Wi-Fi configuration for personal computers.



* Customer guidance on basic computer use and maintenance.



##### \### Out of Scope



* Repair or replacement of severely damaged computer hardware that requires specialized repair facilities.



* Recovery of data from physically damaged or failed storage devices requiring professional laboratory services.



* Installation or repair of personal business network infrastructure.



* Server maintenance, installation, or administration



* Enterprise-level cybersecurity services or security investigations.



* Recovery of passwords or unauthorized access to accounts or devices.



* Sale of computer hardware, software, or other equipment unless specifically included in a separate agreement.



* Disposal or recycling of electronic equipment.



* No maintenance after agreed upon period of service



* Services for devices that are not personally owned or authorized by the customer.

### 

#### \## Deliverables



* Fully configured and operational personal computers.



* Installed and updated operating systems and required software.



* Completed hardware installations or replacements.



* Resolved hardware and software issues.



* Optimized computer performance and system settings.



* Configured printers, peripherals, and Wi-Fi connections.



* Basic security and antivirus configuration.



* Documentation of all services and maintenance provided has been completed.



* provided maintenance and upgrade recommendations.



#### \## Assumptions



* Customers will provide technicians with information about their computer and the problems they are experiencing.



* Customers will provide access to their computers when required.



* Customers will be using authorized operating systems and software (including required software and drivers).



* Technician assumes customer's computer hardware is repairable or can be replaced.



* Customers are required to back up important personal files before techs complete major repairs or system changes.



* Techs will use standard computer repair tools and software to complete services.



* Service completion times are based on the condition of the computer and availability of replacement parts.



* If necessary, customers will provide a safe and suitable environment for on-site services.





#### \## Constraints



* Services must be completed within the agreed-upon timeframe.



* The project will not exceed customer's approved budget.



* Availability of replacement hardware and computer parts may potentially affect completion times.



* Some repairs may be limited due to the condition, complexity, or compatibility of the customer's computer.



* Service availability may be limited by the technicians' schedules.



* Internet access may be necessary for software downloads, updates, troubleshooting, and activation.



* Data recovery and restoration may be limited because of the storage device's condition or cloud space.



* Properly licensed and supported software is required for installation.



* Customer's privacy and confidentiality will be maintained at all times when accessing personal files and accounts.



* Any work completed outside of the agreed project scope requires customer approval before it can be performed.





### \## Acceptance Criteria



###### \### The project will be considered complete when:

### 

* All requested in-scope computer services have been completed.



* Customer's computer is  properly functioning after service(s) are performed.



* The required operating system updates, drivers, and software have been installed or updated as agreed.



* Hardware and peripheral devices included in the service are properly installed and functioning.



* Identified hardware or software problems have been resolved or documented if they cannot be resolved.



* Basic security measures, such as antivirus software and system updates, have been completed when included in the service.



* Requested data backups or file transfers have been completed and verified when applicable.



* The customer has been informed of any remaining issues or recommended maintenance.



* A service report has been completed and provided to the customer.



* The customer has reviewed and verified that work has been satisfactorily completed.

### 

### 

### \## Project Exclusions

### 

### 

* Major hardware repairs that require specialized tools, equipment, or manufacturer service.



* Recovery of data from physically damaged storage devices requiring specialized laboratory equipment.



* Development, customization, or programming of software applications.



* Installation or administration of business servers and enterprise networks.



* Advanced cybersecurity investigations, penetration testing, or forensic services.



* Recovery or bypassing of passwords for accounts or devices without proper authorization.



* Installation of pirated, unlicensed, or illegally obtained software.



* Purchase of replacement hardware or software unless specifically approved by the customer.



* Disposal or recycling of old computer equipment.



* Long-term computer monitoring or maintenance beyond the agreed service period.



* Repair or servicing of equipment that the customer does not own or have authorization to access.



* Any additional services not identified in the approved project scope.





\## Any feature, integration, or requirement not explicitly identified as in scope will require review and approval through the project's change-control process before being added to the project.
