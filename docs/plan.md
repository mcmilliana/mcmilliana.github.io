# Project Plan: McMillian's Small Computer Repair Business Optimization

This project plan outlines the strategic roadmap that will be used to effectively optimize operations, improve turnaround times, and mitigate operational risks for McMillian's small computer repair business.

---

## 1. Risk Analysis (TAME Framework)

The TAME framework addresses business risks by choosing to **Transfer**, **Accept**, **Mitigate**, or **Eliminate** each specific threat.

###  Operational Risks
* **Data Loss during Repairs**
  * **Framework Action:** Mitigate
  * **Strategy:** Require all customers to sign a data waiver which implements a mandatory "backup before repair" protocol for all devices 

###  Supply Chain Risks
* **Parts Shortages or Shipping Delays**
  * **Framework Action:** Transfer & Mitigate
  * **Strategy:** Establish accounts with multiple parts suppliers. I will transfer the cost of expedited shipping to customers who want a quick turn around.

###  Legal & Compliance Risks
* **Customer Data Privacy**
  * **Framework Action:** Mitigate
  * **Strategy:** Enforce strict data-handling policies such as storing devices and drives in a locked space when not actively being worked on. 
---

## 2. Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

This simple task schedule outlines the sequential phases required to set up and optimize the repair workshop.

### Phase 1: Infrastructure & Workshop Setup

  * Install heavy-duty workbenches for work.
  * Deploy ESD grounding mats and wiring to prevent electrical damage.
  * Deploy toolkits and diagnostic monitors to monitor work.
  * Install Network & Storage Devices for data recovery
  * Install and configure isolated technician VLAN (Virtual Local Area Network) to handle infected devices safely.
  

### Phase 2: Software & System Integration
  * Use ticketing software to keep track of repairs
  * Design and upload intake forms and legal waivers for customers' equipment.
  * Inventory Management**
  * Input baseline inventory for common replacement parts (SSDs, RAM, thermal paste).
  * Create a barcode system for tracking parts that are assigned to specific tickets.

### Phase 3: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Launch
  **3.1 Workflow Documenting**
  * Document the 3-step intake process (Triage, Check-in, Waiver signing).
  * Create QA (Quality Assurance) checklists for completed repairs.
  * Staff Training & Dry Run**
  * Train technicians on ticketing updates and backup workflows.
  * Run a mock repair cycle from intake to customer payout to verify systems.
